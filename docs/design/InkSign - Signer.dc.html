Acme Industries · InkSign Ltd · Confidential

Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This agreement is made between Acme Industries ("Acme") and InkSign Ltd ("InkSign"), each of which may disclose confidential information to the other in the course of evaluating a possible commercial relationship.

1 · Confidential information

Confidential information means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

2 · Obligations of the receiving party

The receiving party will use the disclosing party's confidential information only to evaluate the possible relationship, will protect it with at least the care it applies to its own confidential information, and will not disclose it to any third party except to employees and advisers who need it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

Mutual NDA · page 2

3 · Exclusions

These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to it without restriction, is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's information, or is received from a third party entitled to disclose it.

4 · Term

This agreement runs for two years from the date of the last signature below. The confidentiality obligations survive for a further three years after that, and indefinitely for anything that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law.

5 · Return of materials

On written request, the receiving party will return or destroy all confidential information in its possession, save for one archival copy retained solely to evidence compliance with this agreement and any copies held in routine backups.

Mutual NDA · page 3

6 · No licence

Nothing in this agreement grants either party any right or licence in the other's intellectual property, and no obligation to enter into any further agreement is created or implied by these discussions.

7 · No warranty

All confidential information is provided as is. Neither party warrants its accuracy or completeness, and neither is liable for any decision the other takes in reliance on it.

8 · Remedies

The parties agree that damages alone may be an inadequate remedy for a breach of this agreement, and that either party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available to it at law.

Mutual NDA · page 4 · execution

9 · Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

10 · Entire agreement

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on this subject and replaces anything said or written before it. It may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original.
